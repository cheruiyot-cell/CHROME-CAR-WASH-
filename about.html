From frustration to a better standard.

You've been to car washes that left swirl marks, missed spots, and made you nervous about leaving your car. We built CHROME to be the opposite – a calm, secure, premium car-care studio where your car is treated with respect.

What started as a simple idea in Donholm — "what if car washing felt premium and safe?" — became a fully equipped detailing studio. No shortcuts. No guesswork. No stress. Just trained hands, quality products, and a calm environment where you can watch your car being cared for.

Today, CHROME serves busy professionals, families, car enthusiasts, and corporate fleets across Nairobi Eastlands. And we still treat every car like it's our own.

Why we do what we do.

To give Nairobi car owners a car wash experience they can trust — one that is premium, safe, and consistent. We exist to protect your car, respect your time, and remove the stress from car care.
