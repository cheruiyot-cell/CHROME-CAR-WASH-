Every service, explained

We don't rush, and we don't cut corners. Here's what each service includes.

🚿 Express Shine

Our signature quick wash for busy professionals. Your car gets a thorough hand wash using the two-bucket method, followed by a microfibre dry and tyre shine. We also vacuum the interior and clean the windows.

  • Two-bucket hand wash – prevents swirl marks
  • Microfibre towel drying – no water spots
  • Interior vacuum & window cleaning
  • Tyre shine for that fresh look

Ideal for: regular maintenance, pre-meeting, or post-weekend clean-up.

Book Express Shine

🧽 Interior Revival

Go beyond the surface. We deep clean every corner of your car's cabin – seats, carpets, dashboard, door panels, and headliner. We remove stains, eliminate odours, and apply UV protection to keep your interior looking new.

  • Hot water extraction for carpets & seats
  • Steam cleaning for hard surfaces
  • Odour neutralisation (not just masking)
  • UV protection on dashboard & trim

Perfect for: families with kids, pet owners, or anyone who wants a fresh-smelling cabin.

Book Interior Revival

✨ Signature Detail

Our most comprehensive service. This is a complete inside-out transformation. We start with a deep clean of the exterior, follow with clay bar treatment to remove bonded contaminants, and finish with a high-quality carnauba wax. Inside, we perform a full interior detail.

  • Exterior: hand wash, clay bar, machine polish (optional)
  • Sealant or carnauba wax for gloss & protection
  • Full interior deep clean + leather or fabric conditioning
  • Trim restoration & wheel deep clean

Best for: enthusiasts, pre-sale preparation, or special occasions.

Book Signature Detail

🛡️ Ceramic Coating

Long-lasting paint protection that makes washing easier and keeps your car glossy for years. We decontaminate the paint, apply a professional ceramic coating (9H hardness), and provide after-care guidance.

  • Paint decontamination & light polish
  • Professional application of high-grade ceramic
  • Hydrophobic effect – water and dirt slide off
  • UV and chemical resistance

Ideal for: new cars, premium paint, or anyone who hates washing often.

Request Quote

⚙️ Engine Bay Cleaning

We carefully degrease and clean the engine bay without using high-pressure water that can damage electronics. The result is a clean, professional-looking engine that's easier to inspect and maintain.

  • Gentle degreasing with safe products
  • Careful rinsing & drying
  • Dressing on plastic and rubber parts
  • Protects against corrosion

Great for: used car preparation, pre-sale inspections, or peace of mind.

Book Engine Bay Cleaning

🪑 Leather Care

Leather is a luxury material that needs specific care. We clean, condition, and protect your leather seats and interior surfaces to keep them soft, supple, and free from cracks.

  • Leather-specific cleaning – lifts dirt without damage
  • Conditioning to restore natural oils
  • UV protection to prevent cracking & fading
  • Final buff for a soft sheen

Perfect for: premium vehicles, older leather that needs revival, or regular upkeep.
